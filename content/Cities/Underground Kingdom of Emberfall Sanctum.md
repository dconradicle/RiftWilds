### The plains of Ash
The dessert of stone. A slight gust swirls across the plains kicking up ash. It seems like nothing else moves. The air is dry, stale and dusty. [[Ashstarved]] roam here, looking for food? stone? no one knows what they are looking for. The guardian never moves unless it needs to. Somehow the [[Ashstarved]] never attract its wrath but other living things do, including you.

### Outer city
This settlement is barely holding on. Crops need to be harvested early or they turn to stone, just like the people. The people slowly turn to stone, slowly at first. Skin flaky, eyes glaze over then they just leave the settlement off to look for who knows in the plains.
