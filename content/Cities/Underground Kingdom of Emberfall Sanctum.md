### The plains of Ash
The dessert of stone. A slight gust swirls across the plains kicking up ash. It seems like nothing else moves. The air is dry, stale and dusty. [[Ashstarved]] roam here, looking for food? stone? no one knows what they are looking for. The guardian never moves unless it needs to. Somehow the [[Ashstarved]] never attract its wrath but other living things do, including you.

### Outer city
This settlement is barely holding on. Crops need to be harvested early or they turn to stone. The people slowly turn to stone. Skin flaky, eyes glaze over then they just leave the settlement off to look for who knows in the plains.

### Inner City
No one goes in and leaves sane, or at all. 
The place is in pristine condition. The marble walls glitter in the sunlight as though recently polished. The road holds no dirt or debris, as though just swept.
It's the shadows, the things just outside of your view that make your danger sense tingle. Something is there, something evil that you just cant see, but it see's you.
