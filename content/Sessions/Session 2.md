You wake up to the remnants of an explosion all around you. Train bits everywhere, luggage cluttering the ground. 
You look around and you can see that you have fallen into a huge cavern. You are on the edge of a cliff with no way to get out. You look out over the cliff and see this huge cavern has a castle and outskirts in the distance. It is lit up by the occasional sun beam pouring through some cracks in cave roof.

Something catches your eye as an odd structure attached to the castle. The tallest tower in the center reaches all the way to the cave roof and possible past? its hard to tell but maybe just maybe if you can climb to the top of that tower you can get out.

But the first step of this journey is how to get down off the cliff? 

Upon reaching the bottom of the cliff you notice that the ground is a hard igneous rock with a slight film of sand covers the vast plains ahead of you. Upon further inspection  of the area it looks like a huge wave of lava crashed into some sort of magic bubble? protecting half the kingdom but completely burring it under the igneous rock, sealing it from the rest of the world.

This journey across the plains feels like it could take a couple of days.

Nearing the end of the 1st day you notice petrified rodent's and birds. They look like they were all in a rush trying to escape something and running towards the kingdom.

As you journey on, you will have to go down some shallow cliffs, as you get you notice someone? a creature? notice you and scurry over the side.

A skinny Halfling with a spear is resting near the gate as you approach with the guardian on your heals. He wakes looking at you approach not seemingly caring about the guardian. He looks back and calls out and soon more enlightened races wielding spears come out and watch.
