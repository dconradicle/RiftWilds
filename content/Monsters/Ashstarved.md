## Ashstarved

_“They’re not dead… not fully. Just… not stone either”_

---

###  Description

At a distance, an Ashstarved might be mistaken for a person.

Up close, that is no person.

Their skin appears cracked and dry, like stone that has begun to split under pressure. In places, flesh has hardened completely—fingers chipped, joints stiff, parts of the face frozen in expressions that no longer change. Half or parts of their form has turned to stone, like a disease slowly spreading

They crouch low, often digging through rubble, roots, or old debris with desperate focus. When startled, they don’t stand and run—they _scramble_, using hands and feet alike, moving across terrain in ways that seem almost animal. Like a half turned to stone animal limping or scurrying away.

Most carry scraps:

- Torn cloth
    
- Rusted metal
    
- Fragments of the old city
    

None of it seems valuable. All of it seems important to them.

---

### Behavior

Ashstarved are not hunters. They are survivors.

They:

- Avoid open conflict
    
- Watch from a distance
    
- Flee when seen
    
- Strike only when cornered or desperate
    

They prefer uneven ground, cliffsides, and rubble where they can move more easily than others.

If one is spotted, there are often more nearby—watching, waiting, unseen.

---

### What They Want

- Food (of any kind)
    
- Safety
    
- Shelter in broken terrain
    

They scavenge constantly, even from things that no longer resemble food.

---

### Notes

There are signs—subtle, easy to ignore—that these creatures were once people.

A hesitation before fleeing.  
A grip on an object that seems… familiar.  
A face that almost remembers how to emote.

Whether anything remains of who they were is unknown.
